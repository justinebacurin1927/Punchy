# Punchy 0.1.3 — glass island and neon

Download **Punchy-0.1.3-debug.apk** under **Assets** and install it over your current app. Alignment, size, thickness and source choices are kept.

- Redesigned with ROSS: near-black surfaces, neon lime accents, a focused preview/control card and compact settings rows.
- Frosted-glass expanded download/music island with a fine border, smaller layout and matching vector controls.
- Native background blur where supported; automatic darker translucent fallback when Android disables blur. No screen capture or new access needed.
- Same approved launcher icon symbol, now neon lime on black.
- YouTube/video playback no longer automatically forces a music capsule. Only selected music apps can use the wide profile; Muzio, Spotify and YouTube Music are preselected. Adjust **Music capsule apps** for other players. Explicit movie-content sessions are ignored even from a selected app.
- Refreshed alignment and detection screens. Additional setup help and the Quick Settings action are under **More setup options**.

The working v0.1.2 notification reader, paused/playback behavior and calibration keys are retained; media source selection is now explicit. The normal camera center stays black and paused music still returns to the normal shape.

After updating, try a real download, tap the indicator with and without music, and check the new look against your wallpaper. Android 14 emulator checks cover controls, backdrop changes, larger text and updates; the exact HONOR rendering and hardware blur need this visual retest.
