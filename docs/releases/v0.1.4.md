# Punchy 0.1.4 — easier choices and clearer words

Download **Punchy-0.1.4-debug.apk** under **Assets** and install over your current app. Your alignment and app choices are kept.

- New rounded app menus with icons, search and clear selection marks.
- Save keeps your choices; Cancel, Back or tapping outside leaves them as they were.
- A short download-status card replaces the technical text at the bottom of Download detection.
- Easier setup and alignment explanations.
- Removed the unnecessary network note from the main screen.
- Updated the GitHub README with Punchy’s logo and simpler instructions.

The glass island, music-only wide ring and working download reader are kept. Try both app menus, Save/Cancel, and Download detection after updating.
