# Punchy 0.1.1 — music capsule and expandable details

Download **Punchy-0.1.1-debug.apk** from **Assets**. Install it over the existing Punchy app to keep your position, size and thickness settings.

## Changes from your first phone test

- A solid black center in the normal indicator.
- Automatic wide capsule shape when music is playing or paused, with independent music width/height calibration. Its center stays clear so native artwork and the waveform remain visible.
- Tap the indicator to expand a black download card with filename, progress and **Open app**.
- With music detected, the card also shows title/artist, artwork when supplied by the player, and previous/play-pause/next controls.
- Close with X, Back or a tap outside the card. The indicator returns while a download is active.
- Android 14-compatible navigation to the source app's notification action.

Existing notification/accessibility access is reused. No screen-content retrieval, gesture-control capability or network permission was added. No root or Xposed.

## Try on your HONOR

1. Install the update, keep Punchy enabled, and run the preview without music to check the black center.
2. Start music in Muzio, then preview again: the border should use a capsule automatically.
3. If needed, adjust **Shape & alignment → Music capsule fit**. Normal X/Y and thickness apply to both profiles.
4. Tap the indicator to inspect the card and try its controls. Then check with an actual browser download.

Music detection uses Android media sessions; it cannot read HONOR's private capsule dimensions. This is Punchy's own combined popup, and the updated fit and behavior still need your phone test.
